Deck Designer

Design decks that handle flat and sloped yards

A deck planning tool for builders and homeowners across Canada and the US. Draw the yard and how the ground falls away, add decks and stairs, then walk it at eye level. Framing is sized from published span guides, the checks explain themselves, and you finish with printable PDF plans and a priced material list. Free to start, no sign-up needed.

  • Posts and footings sized to the real, sloped ground
  • Framing from AWC DCA 6 and the Canadian Wood Council, with the source page cited
  • Eye-level 3D walk-through
  • PDF plans and a priced material list

Education Live

Times Table Quest

A fun way for kids to learn their times tables

Kids aged 5 to 12 practise multiplication by playing: they pick a buddy, win back eleven lost crystals and explore the Sky Kingdom one island at a time. Behind the fun, every answer shapes what comes next. Missed facts come back sooner with a friendly mini-lesson, and learned facts come back later to make sure they stick. Parents see exactly which facts their child knows. The first three islands are free.

  • Every answer shapes what comes next
  • A fact only counts as learned after it's remembered on different days
  • Parent dashboard showing all 66 facts
  • No ads, no tracking, first 3 islands free
